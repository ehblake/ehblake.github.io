Museum of Old + New Art

Hobart, Australia

Museum of Old and New Art (MONA) is a museum in Hobart founded by David Walsh, known for its provocative mix of ancient, modern, and contemporary art. Built largely underground on the banks of the Derwent River, it presents art through immersive, unconventional exhibitions that challenge traditional museum expectations.

Since 2018, I’ve worked with Mona across a range of projects exploring how people encounter the museum and its ideas. As Design Director, I’ve focused on the visitor experience, bringing structure and coherence to ambitious, often unconventional projects.

For the proposed Mona Hotel, we led on-site research, stakeholder interviews, and experience mapping, synthesising learnings into a detailed experience plan and report delivered directly to the museum’s founder.

For the Mona exhibition MINE, we worked with artist Simon Denny to shape the visitor experience across the exhibition. While the curatorial vision was his, we defined how audiences would move through, interpret, and navigate the work.

Using 3D models, we developed the spatial concept and visitor experience plan, aligning narrative, movement, and interpretation into a cohesive whole.

We also designed and delivered many of the screen-based and interactive elements throughout the exhibition, including integration with Mona’s O device.
